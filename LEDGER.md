## Rules this ledger operates under

1. **No advance.** No category is drawn before the gate it belongs to has been
   accepted in writing.
2. **No external commitment without prior approval** of scope, identity, rate,
   deliverable, and acceptance test.
3. **No category transfer** without written approval; unspent authority is not
   owed to the applicant and is simply not drawn.
4. **Forbidden uses, permanently:** personal crypto trading, token issuance,
   liquidity provision, marketing, third-party custody, or undisclosed
   subcontractors.
5. **Independence is not purchasable.** Review funds cannot be conditioned on a
   favourable conclusion. Reviewer identity, scope, findings status, and retest
   evidence are disclosed whatever the outcome.
6. **Every entry names its gate.** A row without an accepted milestone and a
   repository tag is not a valid entry.
